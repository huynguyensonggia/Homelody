Work at Homelody – a cool, quiet upstairs room in Hoi An

Need somewhere cool to open your laptop, study or take a call? Our upstairs room is air-conditioned and quiet, with free Wi-Fi, about 1.5 km from Hoi An Old Town.

DirectionsMessage on Zalo

Why it works for a work day

  • Air-conditioned upstairs room, away from the garden below
  • Free Wi-Fi and free iced tea
  • Open 6:30am–10pm every day
  • No time limit – stay as long as you like
  • Take a break in the garden by the koi pond
  • Free parking

What to order

Vietnamese coffee, an americano or a latte in the morning; fruit tea or fresh juice later on. Peckish? Try our homemade Brazilian cheese bread or a croissant.

Plan your visit

The upstairs room has just 6–7 seats, so if you need a spot, call or message +84 935 983 263 (Zalo) ahead, or message us on Facebook. We take cash and bank-transfer QR; no cards yet.
